# All Recursive · Financial Influx Flood

Spellwork's Temaline Valid Quilt adapts the Final Quilt and Flood Fever lore into a fictional accounting simulation. One influx carries a quantity in a single fictional unit through a directed network of recursive records. The routing and ledger procedure defines the meaning of a seal in this adaptation.
